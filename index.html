HELP & FAQ

Answers before you have to ask.

What comes in a WorldSip box?

Each box contains 6–8 curated beverages based on its theme. Contents can rotate while the flavor direction stays consistent.

Can I buy individual drinks?

Yes. Boxes are the main discovery experience, and individual drinks are available so you can reorder favorites or build your own mix.

Can I order without subscribing?

Yes. Every box can be purchased as a one-time order. Subscription is optional.

How often do subscription boxes ship?

Monthly subscriptions renew once per month and include one rotating themed box.

Can I pause or cancel?

Yes. Subscription settings are designed to make skipping, pausing, and cancelling straightforward.
